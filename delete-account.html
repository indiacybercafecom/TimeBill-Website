Delete Your TimeBill Account

The Delete Guest Account option is available inside the Profile page under the Data Management section. This action permanently removes your guest account and related app data from TimeBill and should only be completed when you are sure you no longer need the service.

What Delete Account Means

The Delete Guest Account option is available inside the Profile page under the Data Management section. Selecting it removes your TimeBill guest account from the app and permanently deletes the associated account information that was stored for your use of the service, including profile details, saved preferences, and other related app data linked to your account.

What Will Be Deleted

The deletion process permanently removes the account and related information stored by TimeBill, including account profile details, app preferences, usage history, and any account-specific data associated with your guest profile.

warning Premium Warning

This action is permanent and cannot be undone. Once your guest account is deleted, you will not be able to recover the account or its stored information through the app.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the TimeBill app.
  2. Open the left sidebar (Navigation Drawer).
  3. Tap Profile.
  4. Scroll to the Data Management section.
  5. Tap “Delete Guest Account”.
  6. Read the confirmation dialog carefully.
  7. Confirm deletion.
  8. Your guest account and all associated account information will be permanently deleted.

Important Notice

The Delete Guest Account option is available directly inside the TimeBill app from Profile under the Data Management section. If you need help before proceeding, contact support for guidance and confirmation of what will be removed.

Contact Support

If you have questions about account deletion, data removal, or the effect of this action, our support team can help you understand the process before you confirm.
