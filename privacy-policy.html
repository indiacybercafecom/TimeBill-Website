Privacy Policy for TimeBill

This policy explains how TimeBill handles your personal information, device permissions, session data, and user controls in the app.

Information Collection

TimeBill may collect information needed to provide tracking, billing, reporting, and support services. This includes basic account details, session activity, billing preferences, and technical information such as app version and device type.

Permissions

The app may request permissions required for accurate time tracking, such as access to device storage, notifications, or app functionality needed for a smooth experience. These permissions are used only to support the features you choose to use.

Data Storage

TimeBill stores your tracked sessions, billing data, settings, and related records in a secure local environment and through supported application services designed to maintain continuity and backup readiness.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect user data from unauthorized access, misuse, or accidental loss. However, no system can guarantee absolute security, so users should also take care to protect their devices and account access.

User Rights

You have the right to review, update, correct, or remove information that you have provided through the app, subject to legal and operational requirements. You may also choose to stop using the app at any time.

Cookies

The TimeBill website may use cookies or similar technologies to improve performance, remember preferences, and provide a smoother browsing experience. Users may disable cookies in their browser settings, although some pages may function less efficiently.

Third-Party Services

TimeBill may use trusted third-party services for hosting, analytics, support, and app delivery. These services process information only as needed to support the platform and are bound by their own privacy practices.
